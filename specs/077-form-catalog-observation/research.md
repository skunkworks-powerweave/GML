# Research 077
One non-obvious choice: `observation_forms` is response-shaped (FK `cycleId` NOT NULL, unique `(cycleId, kind)`), not catalog-shaped. The brief asks for a feedback_forms-style template seed but explicitly directs us to `observation_forms`. With the schema locked, we park the three templates (pre/post/observer) on the canonical seed cycle `OBS-2026-001` and store the field definitions in the existing `responses` jsonb under a `fields` key. The form UI fetches its template by `cycle.code = 'OBS-2026-001'` + `kind`. Trade-off: the canonical cycle's `observation_forms` rows are reserved for templates, not real teacher submissions — but `OBS-2026-001` is a fixture cycle (seed-only), so no real submissions are at risk. Documented in `designDeviations`.
